# Mockup Background Prompts

Generate each scene with a completely blank, unprinted target surface so logos and brand layouts can be warped cleanly onto the 4-corner plane.
Supported aspect ratios: `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16`.
Each prompt is separated by `---` for easy copying.

**1. Angled Highway Billboard**

Aspect ratio: 4:3.
Prompt:
Photorealistic outdoor photograph looking slightly up at a large rectangular roadside billboard against a clear blue sky with green treetops at the bottom edge.
The billboard has a weathered steel support pole, lower maintenance catwalk, and four small upward-facing floodlights along the bottom frame.
The entire billboard face is completely blank, smooth, matte off-white vinyl with subtle natural daylight shading and no text, no graphics, no logos, and no watermarks.
Crisp 4-corner perspective, shot on 50mm lens, natural midday sunlight.
